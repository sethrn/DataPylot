The first step in any data-driven project is loading the data. 
In DataPylot, this means importing a dataset and converting it into a 
Pandas DataFrame, a foundational data structure used in Python for 
data analysis. DataFrames allow you to explore, manipulate, and 
visualize your data.

Importing a dataset in DataPylot is simple. Click the file browser to upload a 
supported file (.csv, .tsv, .txt, .xlsx, or .json) from your device. If 
you're working in Google Colab, there's an alternative option to mount and 
access your drive, so you can work with files stored in your Google account.

Before finalizing your import, you’ll give your DataFrame a custom name. 
Once imported, your dataset becomes available to all other stages of 
the workflow, unlocking exploration, cleaning, modeling, and more.
For the rest of the session, you'll access your DataFrames by name, and 
DataPylot will keep track of any changes you make to your DataFrames.
