After training a model, the next step is to evaluate how well it performs.
In this section, youll select one of your previously trained models and 
assess its performance using metrics suited to either regression or 
classification tasks.

For regression models, DataPylot offers several commonly used evaluation metrics:
R^2 Score (Coefficient of Determination) measures how well the model explains the 
variance in the target variable. A score closer to 1 indicates a better fit.

Mean Absolute Error (MAE) calculates the average absolute difference between 
predicted and actual values., giving a sense of how far off the model's
predictions are, on average.

Mean Squared Error (MSE) takes the average of squared differences between 
predictions and actual values, penalizing larger errors more heavily than MAE.

Median Absolute Error measures the median of all absolute differences between 
predicted and actual values. It's more robust to outliers than MAE and MSE, 
offering a clearer picture when our data contains extreme values.

For classification models, you'll choose between simple and detailed reporting:
Accuracy measures the proportion of correct predictions out of all predictions.
It's easy to interpret but can be misleading for imbalanced datasets.

Classification Report provides a full breakdown of precision, recall, and 
F1-score for each class. These metrics help you understand not just how many 
predictions were correct, but how well the model handled each specific class.
