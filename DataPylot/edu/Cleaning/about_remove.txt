Not all features in a dataset are useful. As you may have discovered from 
exploring the dataset, some feats may have too many missing values, 
very low variability, or simply aren't relevant to your analysis. 
Removing these features can help simplify your data, reduce noise, and 
improve the performance of your machine learning models.

In this section, you'll choose columns to drop from your DataFrame.
You might remove features that were flagged during earlier analysis, 
such as those with a high number of missing values or those that showed 
no correlation with your target variable. You can select one or more 
features to remove at once, and a new copy of your DataFrame will be 
created without them.

Removing irrelevant or redundant features is a common part of the 
data cleaning and preparation process, helping ensure that your models 
are trained only on the most informative inputs.
