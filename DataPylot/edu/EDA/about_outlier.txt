Outliers are data points that deviate significantly from most of the other 
data points feature. While not always errors, they can distort statistical 
summaries and negatively impact the performance of machine learning models. 
Identifying outliers helps you better understand your data and decide whether 
those points should be removed, corrected, or treated differently.

In this step, you'll choose the type of data to analyze: numerical or categorical. 
For numerical data, you can use either Tukey's Fence or Z-score methods. 

Tukey's Fence identifies outliers as values that fall a certain multiplier 
beyond the interquartile range (IQR). 

Z-score calculates how many standard deviations a value lies from the mean, 
flagging those that exceed a threshold. Both are common and effective for spotting 
unusually high or low values.

For categorical data, DataPylot lets you define a proportional threshold, 
flagging categories that appear infrequently relative to the rest of the dataset. 
This helps highlight rare labels that might lack sufficient representation or 
signal potential data quality issues.
